Meet Monitor

Built to record

The camera comes first.

Choose the capture path that fits your production. Both apps keep the interface direct.

Maximum flexibility

RAW Cam

Capture sensor data for grading, VFX, and finishing workflows where image latitude matters more than file size.

  • CinemaDNG-style DNG sequences and MCRAW
  • Open-gate, 4K, and Full HD capture
  • Manual ISO, shutter, focus, and white balance
  • Histogram, clipping feedback, and Gyroflow GCSV
Get RAW Cam

Ready for the edit

Log Cam

Record compact or edit-ready video from RAW frames with color and exposure tools designed for production.

  • HEVC 4:2:0, HEVC 4:4:4, and ProRes
  • Industry-standard Log gammas, Rec709, HLG, and PQ
  • False color, zebras, gamma assist, and LUT support
  • Open-gate or 4K recording to internal or external storage

Good to know

The camera keeps the footage.

Monitor is a remote view and control surface. The recording iPhone remains the camera and recorder.

Log Cam is required
Install Log Cam on every iPhone you want to use as a recording camera.
Monitor is free
Use Log Cam Monitor on iPhone, iPad, or Mac at no additional cost.
Recordings stay on camera
Media is saved on each recording iPhone, never on the monitoring device.
