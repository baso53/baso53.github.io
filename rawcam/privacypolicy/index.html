2. What RAW Cam does

RAW Cam is an on-device video-camera utility. It works entirely on your iPhone/iPad and has no servers, cloud functions, analytics, advertising or in-app purchases.

3. Personal data we collect

None. RAW Cam does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera & Microphone – used solely to record video when you press the record button.
  • Photos / Files (optional) – only if you choose to save recordings to your device's photo library or Files app.

All recordings remain on your device under your control; RAW Cam never copies them elsewhere.

5. No third-party sharing

Because no data is collected, nothing is shared with third parties.

6. Security

All processing happens locally on the device. Apple's built-in security (device passcode, Face ID, encrypted storage and iCloud backups, if enabled) protects any videos you create.

7. Children's privacy

RAW Cam collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us; however, we do not possess such data.

8. Changes to this policy

If future versions of RAW Cam introduce features that handle personal data, this policy will be updated and you will be notified through the App Store update notes and/or in-app.
